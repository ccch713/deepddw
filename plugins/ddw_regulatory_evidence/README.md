# DDW Regulatory Evidence Plugin

Regulatory document management and compliance evidence chain tracking.

## Features
- **Multi-Jurisdiction**: CN (NHC), EU (Commission/EFSA), US (FDA), International (Codex)
- **Document Types**: Regulations, guidance, approvals, certificates, evidence, reports
- **Evidence Chains**: Link regulatory requirements to company compliance status
- **Gap Analysis**: Track compliance gaps and action plans
- **Pre-built Data**: Seed with core food safety regulations
- **Cabio Template**: Pre-configured evidence chains for Cabio Biotech
